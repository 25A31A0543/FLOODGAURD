Flood Safety & Survival Tips

1. What to Do During Flash Floods
  • Never attempt to walk or drive through flowing water. Just 6 inches of rapid water can knock you down.
  • Move immediately to higher ground or upper building levels.
  • Turn off main electricity switch and LPG gas valves before evacuating.
2. Essential Emergency Grab-Bag Kit
  • Drinking water bottles (at least 3 liters per person).
  • Non-perishable food (biscuits, dates, dry fruits).
  • Waterproof pouch with Aadhaar, ID cards, insurance documents.
  • Flashlight, power bank, whistle, and basic first-aid medications.
3. Electrical & Waterborne Hazards
  • Do not touch electrical equipment or fallen utility lines if wet.
  • Do not drink unfiltered flood tap water. Boil water thoroughly before drinking.
